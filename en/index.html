FAQ

Common questions

What should be installed first?

Content Manager, CSP, Pure and Light Patch. Then apply the pack presets.

Which preset suits an RX 580?

Medium is the recommended starting point. Try Low if FPS is not stable.

My graphics look broken or the game crashes.

Update Pure and Light Patch, check the selected preset, and start from a lighter configuration.
